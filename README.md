# Moody IB & Academic Support Academy

A locally hosted tutoring website designed for families seeking support in George H. Moody IB preparation and academic coaching for grades 1-8 in English, Math, and Science.

## Included features

- Subject-specific hubs for Science, Math, and English
- Family and student portal login flow with TEST credentials
- Instructor-only access with secret word TEST
- Specialty Center for application review and study support
- Cart workflow for tutoring plans and consultation request email draft
- No online payment flow; families are directed to meet and pay in cash after consultation
